Help & Support
Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track a bus?
Open Home or Schedule, select a bus, then tap Track Bus to follow its location on the map.

How do I book a seat?
Use Book a Ride or Book on any schedule card, choose seats, enter details, and confirm (demo payment).

Can I cancel a booking?
Yes — open the booking under My Bookings and use Cancel. Seats are released in the demo.

Is this live GPS?
The app shows live map tracking and ETAs based on the assigned route and trip progress.
